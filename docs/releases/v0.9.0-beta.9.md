# CompDesk v0.9.0-beta.9 — Public Beta (draft)

Help Center suggestions while creating a ticket: link the right articles to a category, and people may find their answer before they submit.

## New: suggested help articles per category

- **Admin → Categories → edit a category → Suggested help articles.** Choose up to three published Help Center articles, in the order they should appear. Super Admins can set them for any category, and department administrators for categories in the departments they administer. Nothing else about a category becomes editable for department administrators.
- **On New Ticket,** after a person picks that category, **This might answer your question** appears between routing and the form.
  - Articles open in a dialog, so what they've entered stays on the page.
  - **This solved it** leaves without creating a ticket, asking first if anything was entered.
  - **Continue with my request** folds the suggestions into a single line.
  - Suggestions never block submitting.
- **Always current.** An article or collection that is unpublished is no longer suggested; deleting an article or category removes its links.
- **No tracking.** CompDesk stays lightweight: nothing is recorded about who read a suggestion or what they did next.
- **Demo data** adds a **Common requests** collection with "Give a colleague access to your mailbox" and "Expense reports: deadlines and receipts". Three demo categories have suggestions.

## API changes

- New: `GET /api/help/suggestions?categoryId=`.
- `PATCH /api/categories` accepts `suggestedArticleIds`, also from department administrators. The admin view of `GET /api/categories` includes `suggestedArticles`.
- `/api/canned-responses` is unchanged and stays available.

## Upgrade and rollback

One additive migration adds the `category_help_articles` table. Back up PostgreSQL, private attachments, uploaded branding and the configuration volume together, then:

```sh
docker compose pull
docker compose up -d
docker compose logs --tail=100 compdesk
```

Rolling back to beta.8 works with the upgraded database: beta.8 ignores the extra table.
