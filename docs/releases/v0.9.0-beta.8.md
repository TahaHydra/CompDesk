# CompDesk v0.9.0-beta.8 — Public Beta (draft)

Know who you are talking to: every name opens a person card, and a new privacy setting decides who sees email addresses and other details.

## New: person cards

- **Hover or click any name** (requester, assignees, message authors, watchers, and names in the ticket list, Department Inbox and dashboard) to open the person's card: photo or initials, name, role (Requester, Agent, Department admin, Super Admin) and department. On phones and with the keyboard, tap or press Enter.
- **Contact and profile details** (email with copy and mailto, preferred language, job title) appear when your organisation's privacy setting allows it.
- **For staff,** the card also shows whether the account is active and a link to the person's open requests (counting only tickets you can access). Super Admins get a link to manage the user.
- **Same names.** Each message shows its author's role ("Requester", "Agent", "Department admin"), and your own messages say "You". If two different people in one ticket share a name, a short hint (part of their email, or their department) is shown next to it.
- **Job title.** Everyone can set an optional job title in **My Profile**; Super Admins can set it in **Admin → Users**.

## New: Admin → Settings → Privacy

- **One setting per field:** email address, preferred language and job title. Each can be visible to **Higher roles only** (default), **Same role and higher**, **All staff**, or **Everyone**.
- **A preview** shows exactly what, for example, a requester sees on an agent's card before you save.
- **Relationship first.** Details are only ever shown for people someone already works with: people on a ticket they can open, or staff in their own departments. A department admin counts as an administrator only for people in the departments it administers. Super Admins always see everything, and everyone sees their own details.
- **One rule everywhere:** cards, ticket pages and lists, assignee pickers, user search and the staff directory. User search no longer matches an email address the searcher is not allowed to see.

## Behaviour change: email addresses are more private by default

Until now, every agent and administrator could see every colleague's email address. With the default **Higher roles only**:

- Department admins still see the agents and requesters of their departments, and agents still see requesters.
- **Agents no longer see other agents' or administrators' email addresses**, and department admins no longer see each other's.
- Assignee pickers show role and department instead of the email when it is hidden.

To keep the previous behaviour, open **Admin → Settings → Privacy** and set **Email address → All staff**. A small internal team can choose **Everyone**, so requesters also see the email of the staff on their tickets.

## API changes

- Hidden contact fields are **omitted** from responses. See [People and privacy](../API_REFERENCE.md#people-and-privacy) for the affected endpoints.
- New: `GET /api/users/[id]/card`, `GET`/`PUT /api/settings/people-privacy`. `PATCH /api/profile/preferences` accepts `jobTitle`.
- `GET /api/tickets/[id]` adds `peopleHints`, and timeline authors include `role`. Requesters now receive assignees' `role` (and `email` when the policy allows).
- The external API (`/api/v1`) is unchanged.

## Upgrade and rollback

One additive migration adds a nullable `job_title` column to `users`. Back up PostgreSQL, private attachments, uploaded branding and the configuration volume together, then:

```sh
docker compose pull
docker compose up -d
docker compose logs --tail=100 compdesk
```

Rolling back to beta.7 works with the upgraded database: beta.7 ignores the extra column and the privacy setting.

## Verification

- Lint, type checking and the full Jest suite pass. New tests cover:
  - the privacy levels and the department-scoped admin rank;
  - safe fallback for an invalid stored policy;
  - card relationship checks (ticket access and membership, no internal-note authors for requesters, 404 for unrelated people and malformed ids);
  - requesters seeing only the ticket's department;
  - the search guard against probing hidden emails.
- The migration matches the Prisma schema, the upgrade rehearsal passes, and the demo lifecycle test passes with job titles on demo accounts.
