# CompDesk v0.9.0-beta.6 — Public Beta (draft)

Know who you're talking to, answer faster, and help people help themselves. This release adds person cards with an organisation privacy setting, personal saved replies for staff, Help Center suggestions while creating a ticket, and tightens who can moderate conversations.

## ⚠ Behaviour change: email addresses are more private by default

Until now, every agent and administrator could see every colleague's email address. The new **Admin → Settings → Privacy** setting defaults to **Higher roles only**:

- Department admins still see the agents and requesters of their departments, and agents still see requesters.
- **Agents no longer see other agents' or administrators' email addresses**, and department admins no longer see each other's.
- Assignee pickers show role and department instead of a hidden email.

**To keep the previous behaviour,** set **Email address → All staff**. A small internal team can choose **Everyone**, so requesters also see the email of the staff on their tickets.

## New: person cards

- **Hover or click any name** to open the person's card: photo or initials, name, role (Requester, Agent, Department admin, Super Admin) and department.
  - This works for the requester, assignees, message authors and watchers, and for names in the ticket list, Department Inbox and dashboard.
  - On phones and with the keyboard, tap or press Enter.
- **Contact and profile details** (email with copy and mailto, preferred language, job title) appear when the privacy setting allows it.
- **For staff,** the card also shows whether the account is active and links to the person's open requests, counting only tickets you can access. Super Admins also get a link to manage the user.
- **Same names.**
  - Each message shows its author's role, and your own messages say "You".
  - If two different people in one ticket share a name, a short hint (part of their email, or their department) appears next to it.
- **Job title.** Everyone can set an optional job title in **My Profile**. Super Admins can set it in **Admin → Users**.

## New: Admin → Settings → Privacy

- **One setting per field:** email address, preferred language and job title. Each can be visible to **Higher roles only** (default), **Same role and higher**, **All staff**, or **Everyone**.
- **A preview** shows what, for example, a requester sees on an agent's card, before you save.
- **Relationship first.** Details are only ever shown for people someone already works with: people on a ticket they can open, or staff in their own departments.
- **Department-scoped admin rank.** A department admin counts as an administrator only for people in the departments it administers.
- **Super Admins always see everything,** and everyone sees their own details.
- **One rule everywhere:**
  - cards, ticket pages and lists;
  - assignee pickers and the staff directory;
  - user search, which no longer matches an email address the searcher may not see.

## New: saved replies (staff only)

- **In the reply box,** a **Saved replies** button sits next to Public reply / Internal note.
  - It lists your replies, most used first, with a search box that matches titles and text.
  - Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click. The text is inserted where your cursor is.
  - Your draft is never replaced and nothing is sent until you press Send.
- **Save current reply** stores what you've typed under a title you choose.
- **My Profile → My saved replies** lets you create, edit, rename and delete them.
- **Personal.** Only you can see or use your saved replies; administrators cannot see other people's. Requesters do not have the feature. Each person can keep up to 200.
- **Existing canned responses keep working.** They appear read-only under **Shared responses** in the same menu.

## New: suggested help articles per category

- **Admin → Categories → edit a category → Suggested help articles.** Choose up to three published Help Center articles, in the order they should appear.
  - Super Admins can set them for any category.
  - Department administrators can set them for categories in the departments they administer; nothing else about a category becomes editable for them.
- **On New Ticket,** after a person picks that category, **This might answer your question** appears between routing and the form.
  - Articles open in a dialog, so what they've entered stays on the page.
  - **This solved it** leaves without creating a ticket, asking first if anything was entered.
  - **Continue with my request** folds the suggestions away.
  - Suggestions never block submitting.
- **Always current.** Unpublished articles or collections are no longer suggested, and deleting an article or category removes its links. Nothing is tracked about who read a suggestion.

## Security and permission fixes

- **Moderation follows the department.**
  - Editing or deleting someone else's reply or internal note now requires administering *that ticket's* department.
  - A department admin who is only an agent elsewhere can no longer moderate there.
  - Only a Super Admin can edit or delete a Super Admin's entries.
  - Your own replies work as before: edit within 24 hours, delete at any time.
- **Attachments.**
  - Requesters can remove only files they uploaded themselves; before, they could remove files an agent attached.
  - Older files with no recorded uploader can no longer be removed by requesters.
  - Staff removal is unchanged.
  - The remove button only appears when the server will allow it.
- **Less staff data for requesters.** The ticket list no longer sends assignee email addresses to requester accounts unless the privacy setting allows it.

## Clearer deletion

- **Deleted replies** show a **Deleted** placeholder to everyone. Staff also see **who deleted it and when**.
- **Super Admins** still see the original text, marked "Original text visible to Super Admins only". Nothing changed in what is stored.
- **Removing an attachment is described as permanent:** the file is deleted and cannot be recovered; only its name, size and who removed it are kept.

## Improvements

- **Ticket tags.** In a ticket's Actions panel, the Tags label sits above a full-width "Add tags" button, lined up with Status, Priority and Category.
- **Ticket details.** Labels and values in the Details panel keep a gap, so a long category name no longer runs into its label.
- **Demo data.**
  - A **Common requests** help collection, with "Give a colleague access to your mailbox" and "Expense reports: deadlines and receipts".
  - Category suggestions on three demo categories.
  - Two saved replies for the IT agent.
  - Job titles on the demo accounts.

## API changes

- **Contact fields hidden by the privacy policy are omitted** from responses (not returned empty). This affects:
  - `GET /api/tickets` and `GET /api/tickets/{id}`;
  - the ticket objects returned by create, update and escalate;
  - `GET /api/users` and `GET /api/users/search`;
  - department-admin views of `GET /api/queues`.

  Super Admin responses are unchanged. See [People and privacy](../API_REFERENCE.md#people-and-privacy).
- **New endpoints:**
  - `GET /api/users/{id}/card`;
  - `GET`/`PUT /api/settings/people-privacy`;
  - `GET/POST /api/saved-replies`, `PATCH/DELETE /api/saved-replies/{id}`, `POST /api/saved-replies/{id}/use`;
  - `GET /api/help/suggestions?categoryId=`.
- **`GET /api/tickets/{id}`:**
  - each timeline entry includes `permissions: { canEdit, canDelete }` and the author's `role`;
  - deleted entries include `deletedBy` for staff (`deletedById` is now null for requesters);
  - attachments include `canDelete`;
  - the ticket includes `conversationPermissions` and `peopleHints`.
- **`PATCH /api/categories`** accepts `suggestedArticleIds`, also from department administrators. **`PATCH /api/profile/preferences`** and **`PATCH /api/users`** accept `jobTitle`.
- **New 403s:** the comment edit/delete and attachment delete endpoints return 403 in the newly restricted cases above.
- **Unchanged:** the external API (`/api/v1`) and `/api/canned-responses`.

## Upgrade and rollback

Three additive migrations run automatically on start:

- the `saved_replies` table;
- a nullable `users.job_title` column;
- the `category_help_articles` table.

No existing data is changed. Back up PostgreSQL, private attachments, uploaded branding and the configuration volume together, then:

```sh
docker compose pull
docker compose up -d
docker compose logs --tail=100 compdesk
```

Rolling back to beta.5.1 works with the upgraded database: beta.5.1 ignores the extra tables, column and setting. Saved replies, job titles and suggestions reappear if you upgrade again.
